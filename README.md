# Anchor-First Pricing Presenter

Presents any price ladder in fixed order: top tier first as the complete outcome, middle as the considered alternative, entry last and narrow on purpose.

A free plugin from KAIROS AI Systems — one focused tool that does one job well for a
small business: no suite to learn, no setup project, no account required beyond your
agent host.

## What it does

The order never inverts for nerves; the second price must read against the first. For sales conversations and proposals — presentation law, not public copy.

## How to use it

Add this plugin to a compatible agent host (Cursor, Grok Bot, or another
agent-plugins host), then ask in plain language, for example:

> Use the Anchor-First Pricing Presenter to help me: presents any price ladder in fixed order: top tier first as the complete outcome, middle as the considered alternative, entry last and narrow on purpose — I'll paste the details.

The plugin works from the facts you give it — your services, your prices, your
records. It never invents a number, a policy, or a result; anything it projects is
labelled an estimate with its basis. Drafts that would go to a customer stay drafts
until you approve them.
